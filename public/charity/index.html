Fox Magic for a Cause is Kaede Kitsune's ongoing effort to turn games, art, and community into tangible support for people living with scoliosis, peripheral neuropathy, muscular dystrophy, and related neuromuscular conditions.

The causes

Three communities.
One shared purpose.

These are organizations Kaede chooses to support. Kaede Kitsune is not currently affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by these organizations unless a specific campaign says otherwise.

Scoliosis

National Scoliosis Foundation

A patient-led nonprofit focused on scoliosis awareness, early detection, education, patient support, advocacy, and research.

How giving works

Direct, transparent,
and community-first.

Whenever possible, donations will go directly through the selected nonprofit or its approved fundraising platform rather than through Kaede Kitsune.

For special charity streams, we'll clearly name the organization being supported, the fundraising goal, where the money goes, and the verified total raised afterward.

Our first dedicated Fox Magic for a Cause stream is being prepared now. Campaign links and the event date will be posted here once the fundraising routes are verified.
