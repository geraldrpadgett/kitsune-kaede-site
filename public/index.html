✦ The story

From shrine ink
to digital worlds.

Kaede is an Edo-era kitsune shrine artist who once used foxfire to bring drawings and charms briefly to life. After being accidentally transported into the modern world, they become fascinated by the strange new “magic” humans have created through digital art, 3D modeling, and video games.

Now Kaede moves between a traditional shrine form and a modern gamer form while learning how far creativity—and technology—can take them.

01

Shrine Spirit Form

Edo foxfire

Kaede’s more traditional shrine-spirit form, with long hair, flowing robes, nine tails, and foxfire magic. This version leans into their Edo-era origins, art, mysticism, and autumn kitsune aesthetic.

02

Modern Gamer Form

Digital kitsune

A modern-day kitsune gamer with a playful, laid-back vibe. Short hair and streetwear reflect Kaede’s adaptation to technology, gaming, and the digital world.
